How to refer a patient

01

Write the prescription

Include the product or compression class. We'll confirm details with you if anything's unclear.

02

Send it over

Fax, email, or have your patient bring it in. We log it and reach out to schedule.

03

We measure or scan, verify benefits, and place the order — keeping you updated along the way.

04

We check fit, adjust as needed, and let your office know the patient is taken care of.

Have questions?

How do I refer a patient?
Write a prescription with the product or compression class and send it over by fax, email, or in person. We take it from there.
Which insurance plans do you accept?
We're contracted with most major plans, including Medicare, Medicaid, and many commercial insurers. We verify your patient's specific benefits before placing any order.
Do you handle custom orders?
Yes. We offer custom compression garments, Radiant Impressions custom breast forms, and other specialty fittings. All custom work is done in-house at our Cedar Park location.
Will you keep my office updated?
Yes. We update your office at key steps — after the fitting, when the order ships, and after the patient's follow-up appointment. You'll know your patient is taken care of.
