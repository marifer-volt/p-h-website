What we fit

Compression Garments For

Head & Neck

Chin straps, neck wraps, and capeline hoods for patients managing lymphedema following head and neck cancer treatment or other head and neck conditions. Custom-fit to your measurements for optimal compression and comfort.

Torso: Bras, vests, and swell pads

Compression bras, vests, camisoles, and swell spots for managing truncal lymphedema and post-surgical swelling. Available in soft and firmer compression levels, fitted to your prescription.

Arms & Hands: Arm sleeves, gloves, gauntlets

Sleeves and gauntlets that manage swelling in the arm and hand. Off-the-shelf or custom-knit to your measurements and your physician's prescribed compression class.

Legs and feet: Leggings, thigh-high, calf, foot/ankle

Stockings, leggings, capris, and pantyhose for managing swelling in the legs and feet. Available in knee-high, thigh-high, and full-leg styles, in standard and custom sizes to match your prescription compression class.

we're here to help

What is your exchange and remake policy?
Items billed to insurance can't be returned. If an off-the-shelf garment doesn't fit, we may be able to exchange it for a different size within 30 days of receipt, though some manufacturers charge a restocking fee. If a custom garment doesn't fit as intended, we'll work with the manufacturer to remake it for no additional charge — we just need you to bring it in within 2 weeks of receipt so we can address any fit issues promptly.
What is the difference between ready-to-wear and custom garments?
Ready-to-wear garments are manufactured in standard sizes, while custom garments are made specifically to your measurements. If a standard size cannot provide the proper fit or support, a custom garment may be recommended. Our fitters will help determine the best option based on your measurements and clinical needs.
Why is a professional fitting important?
Compression therapy is most effective when the garment fits correctly. Our experienced fitters hand-measure and evaluate your individual needs to recommend the right garment, compression level, and fit for your condition. The result is a personalized solution designed for the best possible fit, comfort, and function.
